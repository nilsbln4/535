Built beats building.

When new AI data centers get harder to build, the capacity that already exists becomes more valuable, and the companies that still have to build it, with borrowed money, lose.

§1Thesis

AI demand has not slowed. What has changed is the cost of adding capacity. Local voters, state moratoria and tariffs that make data centers pay for their own grid upgrades all raise the price of each new megawatt. Barclays calls it a shift from a race to a "war of attrition," where scarce power favours the largest balance sheets. Two groups come out of this differently:

Long · already built

Hyperscalers and data-center landlords with energized capacity. Their capex is a fraction of revenue and their interest bill is small.

    Short · still building

    GPU clouds and developers whose growth depends on new sites funded with debt. Capex is several times revenue, and interest takes up to a quarter of it.

      The position is beta-neutral: the short basket is sized so that both sides move the same amount with the Nasdaq-100. It is a bet on the gap between the two groups, not on AI stocks going up or down.

      Predictions

      1. The spread is positive over 6 and 12 months from inception.
      2. It rises on news that restricts new data centers and falls on news that overrides local rules.
      3. Its return is not explained by the market: beta to QQQ stays near zero.

      Wrong if

      1. The spread falls 15% from its high since inception.
      2. The short basket beats the long basket by 25 points since inception.
      3. Federal rules override local and state data-center limits.
      4. GPU clouds refinance cheaply, for example high-yield notes well below 10%.

      Why politics turned: the power bill

      Since January 2021 electricity prices have risen faster than prices overall. That gap is what voters punished in November, and it is what tariffs that make data centers pay for their own grid upgrades are designed to close.

      The election, two sessions

      Day one rewarded what is already built. On day two everything fell, and the hedged spread was flat: the market was pricing a risk to AI as a whole, not yet the split.

        §5Method

        Baskets
        Equal weight within each side, rebalanced daily. Price returns, before dividends.
        Hedge
        Short dollars per $1 long = beta of the long basket ÷ beta of the short basket, both against QQQ over the previous 60 sessions. Set at inception, then on the first session of each month, using only data from before that day.
        Not modelled
        Stock borrow fees, financing and commissions. Borrow on heavily shorted GPU-cloud stocks can be expensive, so the live spread overstates what a real short would earn.
        Data
        Prices: daily bars from the course server (Interactive Brokers) with LSEG as the fallback. Fundamentals: LSEG. Electricity and CPI: FRED. Only basket-level indices and ratios are published, never raw vendor prices.
        Pipeline
        The course server pushes daily bars to this repository. A GitHub Action recomputes the live spread after every push and every weekday evening, then commits the result. This page reads it.
